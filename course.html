Assignment MOOCs Evaluation (Certificate + Test)

Dear students, all of you must submit the “ MOOCs Evaluation (Certificate + Test)” https://www.coursera.org/learn/intro-self-driving-cars/ 2. https://www.udemy.com/course/applied-deep-learningtm-the-complete-self-driving-car-course/ 3. https://www.classcentral.com/course/intro-self-driving-cars-13140

Assignment MOOCs Review

A. MOOC Course Overview – 10 Marks

  1. State the title, platform, instructor and duration of the MOOC.
  2. Explain the major objectives of the MOOC.
  3. Explain how the MOOC is related to the Self Driving Cars course.

B. Key Concepts Learned – 15 Marks

  1. Explain any three important concepts learned from the MOOC.
  2. Describe the importance of autonomous driving technology.
  3. Identify two important technical concepts that you learned.

C. Hardware & Software – 15 Marks

  1. Explain the major hardware components of a self-driving car.
  2. Explain the role of the software stack in autonomous vehicles.
  3. Explain how hardware and software work together.

D. Sensors, Perception & Localization – 20 Marks

  1. Explain the role of cameras in autonomous vehicles.
  2. Explain LIDAR and its applications.
  3. Explain GPS/INS and localization.
  4. Explain object detection and semantic segmentation.

These areas align with the course syllabus, which includes GNSS/INS, LIDAR, visual perception, 2D object detection and semantic segmentation.

E. Vehicle Modeling, Control & Planning – 20 Marks

  1. Explain vehicle dynamic modeling.
  2. Explain longitudinal control.
  3. Explain lateral control.
  4. Explain motion planning and behavior planning.

These topics are explicitly included in the course syllabus.

F. CARLA / Python / Practical Application – 10 Marks

  1. Explain how CARLA can be used for an autonomous-vehicle simulation.
  2. Describe one Python-based application demonstrated or learned through the MOOC.

CO4 specifically includes CARLA and Python programming.

G. Learning Reflection – 10 Marks

  1. What were the three most important things you learned?
  2. Which topic was most useful and why?
  3. How can the knowledge gained from this MOOC be applied to self-driving-car development?
